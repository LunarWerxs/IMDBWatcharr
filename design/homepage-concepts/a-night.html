How it works ›

1
Paste a public IMDb link
Open your watchlist (imdb.com/user/ur.../watchlist/) or a list (imdb.com/list/ls...) on IMDb and copy the address from your browser. Make sure it is set to public, or we cannot read it.
2
Copy your two links
The same IMDb list always gives you the same two links, so you only have to set this up once.
3
Paste them into Radarr and Sonarr
Radarr takes the movies link as an RSS list. Sonarr takes the shows link as a custom list.
Sign in, free, and we check your list about every fifteen minutes.
