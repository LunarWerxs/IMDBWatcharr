Storyline >

1

Paste a public IMDb link. Open your watchlist or a list on IMDb and copy the address from your browser. Make sure it is set to public, or we cannot read it.

2

Copy your two links. The same IMDb list always gives you the same two links, so you only set this up once.

3

Paste them into Radarr and Sonarr. Radarr takes the movies link as an RSS list. Sonarr takes the shows link as a custom list.

FAQ >

Does it cost anything? ▸
No. Watcharr is free.
Why sign in? ▸
Without an account we read your list when you paste it. Sign in and we check it for you about every fifteen minutes.
My list is private ▸
Set it to public on IMDb, then paste the link again.
Why was a show skipped? ▸
Sonarr needs a TVDB id for every show. When we cannot find one, we leave that show out.
